Our Understanding of Your Business

This brief reflects our research into 42Q and Glassdome before kickoff — from your websites, AWS Marketplace listing, and public sources. The goal is to show our work and get alignment on where we have it right and where you'll sharpen us.

42Q · 42-q.com · Primary
Cloud-Native MES on AWS
42Q is a full-featured, cloud-native Manufacturing Execution System delivered as SaaS on AWS — the software layer between ERP/PLM and the shop floor. It enforces production steps, captures quality, and builds full track-and-trace genealogy and electronic Device History Records. A business unit of Fortune 500 Sanmina, with 30+ years of manufacturing heritage across 70–100+ factories. Products: Connected & Smart Manufacturing, the Digital Factory Xcelerator, Arthur AI, OMNI Analytics, and Xconnect IIoT connectivity.
Glassdome · glassdome.com
Verified Product Carbon Footprint
Glassdome is a manufacturing-operations and sustainability platform with two products: Production Monitoring (real-time OEE and downtime) and Product Carbon Footprint (measured, per-unit emissions). Its edge is primary factory data — sub-metering real machines rather than industry averages — for audit-ready reporting under CBAM and the EU Battery Regulation. ISO 14067 verified and Catena-X certified; partners include Siemens (SiGREEN), LG Electronics, and Samsung SDI.
